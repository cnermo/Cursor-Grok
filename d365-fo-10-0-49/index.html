Sandbox this before October 2

Self-update has been available since September 11, 2026. The first production auto-update starts October 2, and the second wave starts November 1. The largest behavior change is not a new screen. Customer and vendor netting, advanced bank reconciliation, and several Inventory Visibility integrations become mandatory and can no longer be turned off.
