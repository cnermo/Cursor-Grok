Application architecture · F&O and Dataverse

System components and integrations

Finance and Operations is the hub. The LVS model sits with Avalara Tax, TAS, and SK Global (SK_Common, SKS_BFC, Bank Fabric). Tempus, Ceridian, and BYOD (selected HRM tables) sit outside as integrations. Synapse Link lands finance data in the Data Lake. Dual-write reaches the Dataverse apps: Project Operations, Expense Management, and Customer Insights Journeys. More applications will be added as they are confirmed.
