Do not price the move from this sheet yet

The two sides are not the same environments. The LCS total includes two paid empty slots. The Power Platform subtotal leaves out its own production row. Cloud-hosted and developer prices step up by 1 from row to row, which is what a filled-down cell does, not what a quoted rate does. Line the names up with the landscape, confirm one rate per tier, and then the comparison is usable. Acceptance at zero is expected: the default Dynamics 365 package includes production, 20 user licenses, and one sandbox.

What is usable for the move

Acceptance stays at zero in any comparison. The default Dynamics 365 package includes production, 20 user licenses, and one sandbox, and that sandbox is LVS-FO-ACCEPTANCE. The empty Tier 2 slots are the clearest LCS cost that does not have to move: 2,400 per month, 28,800 per year, with no environment attached. Replace the stepped 750 and 1,003 series with one confirmed rate each, put DEV-12 on its own row, and include the production row in the Power Platform sum. Then rebuild the list from the 14 current environments instead of the names in this workbook.
