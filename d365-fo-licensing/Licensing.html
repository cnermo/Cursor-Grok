Same counts as the license summary

149 with no license and 47 under-licensed match the tenant license summary from the same morning. 179 people are aligned. The summary’s 241 over-licensed users is the wider bucket: it also treats a Team Member license as more than Human Resources Self Service. 112 people whose only production requirement is self-service hold Team Member. Adding those 112 to the 130 spare full licenses lands on 242.

How a license covers a role

  • Finance Premium covers Finance.
  • Every full user license covers Team Members, Operations Activity, and Human Resources Self Service.
  • Team Members covers Human Resources Self Service.
  • A full license does not cover a different product’s full role.

Rules are from the March 2026 Dynamics 365 Licensing Guide, Appendix F.
