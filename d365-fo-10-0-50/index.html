Not this release

Visual Studio 2022 support for X++ ends at platform update 75, which is 10.0.51, not 10.0.50. The 10.0.49 platform article states that only Visual Studio 2026 is supported from PU75.

The client and server method keywords are warnings as of 10.0.48. The X++ language reference still says they are ignored and do not fail compilation.

Starts at 10.0.50

Purchase order line imports that fail with "Updates not allowed for PurchaseOrderNumber" can use the standard purchase order consistency check from 10.0.50 onward. The check looks for lines with a blank inventory transaction ID. Fix mode deletes those lines in the current legal entity when they also have no source document line reference.

On a version earlier than 10.0.50, Microsoft tells you to stop retrying the import and contact support. Run detection before Fix error.

Still only on the platform article for 10.0.49

Batch job occurrence telemetry remains documented for PU74 / 10.0.50 at platform build 7.0.8219.0 and later. There is still no platform what's-new article for 10.0.50.

That telemetry was backported. It is already available on 10.0.49 at platform build 7.0.8199.5 and later, and on 10.0.48 at 7.0.7996.85 and later, if flight BatchJobOccurrenceTelemetryFlight is on.
